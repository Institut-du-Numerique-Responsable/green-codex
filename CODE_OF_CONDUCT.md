# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Green Codex to be a welcoming, respectful, and evidence-driven project for contributors
of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and assume good faith.
- Welcome corrections and make room for different accessibility needs.
- Protect confidential information and personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing
private information without consent are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately through the security contact channel or to a project maintainer.
Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.
