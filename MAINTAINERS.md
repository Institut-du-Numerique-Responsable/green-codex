## Becoming a Maintainer

We welcome new maintainers who:
- Have made **significant contributions** to the project (code, docs, or community).
- Demonstrate **expertise** in Green IT, accessibility, or responsible AI.
- Are committed to **evidence-based** and **non-dogmatic** guidance.

### Process
1. Contribute regularly to the project (PRs, issues, reviews).
2. Express interest to an existing maintainer.
3. The core team will review your contributions and discuss alignment.
4. If approved, you will be added as a **CODEOWNER** in `.github/CODEOWNERS`.
